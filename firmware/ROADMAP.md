# OffGrid Messenger Roadmap (November 2026)

- **Frozen stable baseline:** 1.4.10.2 InterruptUSB (measured on real hardware).
- **Latest known working experimental UI:** 1.4.11.1 ManualLR (user confirmed working).
- **This release:** 1.4.11.2 RF Lab; experimentally compare LR at lower real TX power and simulate RSSI to test auto rate choice; not a substitute for outdoor range testing.
- **Next:** Verify automatic mode and recovery at real weak-link conditions; optimize measured throughput if needed without modifying stable encrypted text/ACK paths.
- **Later:** Encrypted voice notes, notifications/reconnect polish, then Android Studio/Kotlin mobile app last.
- **Out of scope:** GPS and location sharing (user removed).
