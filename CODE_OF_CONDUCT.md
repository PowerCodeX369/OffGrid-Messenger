# Community conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Discuss designs and bugs without personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, doxxing or threats. Respect contributors' privacy and do not publish logs, keys or addresses without consent.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through an appropriate private channel published on the repository profile. Until a contact is published, do not post sensitive concerns publicly.

Maintainers may moderate discussions, close inappropriate reports and restrict participation. This policy does not replace applicable platform policies.
